# Reel Digest

A personal system that turns short self-improvement and AI videos I share while scrolling into one weekly article with steps I can act on.

## Language

### Capturing

**Reel**:
One short video I share in (TikTok, Instagram Reel or YouTube Short), identified by its link.
_Avoid_: Video, post, clip, TikTok

**Inbox**:
The private Telegram bot I share Reels into. It is the only way a Reel enters the system.
_Avoid_: Group chat, channel, share file

**Receipt**:
The one-line reply the Inbox sends after a share, confirming the Reel was saved or could not be read. It never contains a summary.
_Avoid_: Confirmation, reply, summary

**Note**:
Text I type alongside a Reel's link when sharing it, saying what I care about in it.
_Avoid_: Comment, message, caption

**Failed Reel**:
A Reel that could not be read after one retry. It is listed at the end of the Digest so I can rewatch it myself.
_Avoid_: Error, broken link

**Transcript**:
The spoken words of a Reel, as text.
_Avoid_: Captions, subtitles

**No-speech Reel**:
A Reel with no usable spoken words (music with on-screen text). It is summarised from its caption and hashtags and flagged as such.
_Avoid_: Silent reel, empty reel

### Reading

**Category**:
One of a fixed set of topic buckets each Reel belongs to: AI & Tech, Self-Help & Mindset, Socialising & Relationships, Health & Fitness, Money & Career, Other.
_Avoid_: Tag, topic, label

**Action Step**:
One concrete thing to do, drawn from a Reel ("how to actually do it").
_Avoid_: Tip, takeaway

**Digest**:
The weekly article covering the last 7 days of Reels, grouped by Category, delivered by email on Sunday at 9 AM.
_Avoid_: Summary, newsletter, report

**Narration**:
An audio version of the Digest. Not built yet: for now I use my phone's read-aloud.
_Avoid_: Voiceover, podcast
